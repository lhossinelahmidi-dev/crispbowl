## Why Traditional Meal Prep Fails Most People

The biggest mistake beginners make with meal prep is cooking five identical plastic containers of chicken, white rice, and steamed broccoli. By Wednesday, nobody wants to open that container, and by Thursday, you're ordering takeout.

True meal prep success comes from **Component Prep**—cooking flexible building blocks that can be mixed and matched into completely different meals all week.

---

### Step 1: Roast Two Big Sheet Pans of Vegetables
Toss sweet potatoes, bell peppers, broccoli florets, and red onion wedges with olive oil, salt, garlic powder, and paprika. Roast at 400°F (200°C) until golden brown and caramelized. These can top salads, accompany grains, or slide into breakfast tacos.

### Step 2: Cook a Big Batch of Grains
Make a batch of brown rice, quinoa, farro, or couscous. Store in an airtight container with a paper towel tucked under the lid to absorb excess moisture so the grains stay fluffy.

### Step 3: Prep One Versatile Protein
Slow-cook shredded salsa chicken, roast a batch of herb-crusted tofu, or bake seasoned salmon fillets. Season them simply so they fit into burrito bowls, grain bowls, or wraps.

### Step 4: Whisk 2 Bold Sauces
The sauce makes the meal. If you have:
1. A punchy lemon-tahini or garlic-herb vinaigrette
2. A creamy peanut or chipotle-lime crema

You can turn the exact same chicken, roasted sweet potatoes, and quinoa into an Asian-inspired bowl on Monday, and a Mexican fiesta bowl on Tuesday!

---

## Final Tip: Invest in Glass Containers

Glass meal prep containers don't absorb food odors or stains from tomato sauces, they reheat food evenly in the microwave, and they are dishwasher safe.
