## Introduction

Avocado toast is more than just a trend; it's a genuinely delicious, nutritious, and incredibly fast meal. While it seems simple, taking a few extra seconds to season your avocado properly and choosing high-quality bread makes all the difference.

This is my baseline recipe. From here, you can add everything from a poached egg and smoked salmon to roasted cherry tomatoes or a drizzle of hot honey.

## Instructions

1. **Toast the Bread:** Toast your slice of bread until it is golden brown and sturdy enough to hold the toppings. For extra flavor, you can brush the bread with a little olive oil before toasting.
2. **Prepare Avocado:** Cut the avocado in half, remove the pit, and scoop the flesh of one half into a small bowl. (Store the other half with the pit still in it, wrapped tightly in plastic wrap in the fridge).
3. **Mash:** Add the lemon juice to the avocado and mash it with a fork. Leave it slightly chunky for better texture.
4. **Assemble:** Spread the mashed avocado evenly over the toasted bread.
5. **Season:** Sprinkle generously with flaky sea salt, freshly ground black pepper, and a pinch of red pepper flakes if you like some heat. 
6. **Finish:** Finish with a light drizzle of high-quality extra virgin olive oil. Serve immediately.
