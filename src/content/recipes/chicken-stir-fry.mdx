## Introduction

This Easy Chicken Stir-Fry is a weeknight lifesaver. It is significantly faster, healthier, and tastier than ordering takeout. Packed with lean protein and colorful vegetables, it's a balanced meal in one pan.

The secret to a great stir-fry is preparation (mise en place). Because the cooking happens incredibly fast over high heat, make sure to chop all your veggies, cube the chicken, and mix the sauce before you even turn on the stove.

## Instructions

1. **Make the Sauce:** In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, honey (or brown sugar), sesame oil, and cornstarch until smooth. Set aside.
2. **Cook the Chicken:** Heat 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken pieces and cook until browned and cooked through, about 5-7 minutes. Remove chicken from the pan and set aside on a plate.
3. **Stir-Fry Vegetables:** Heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of oil in the same pan. Add the garlic and ginger, cooking for 30 seconds until fragrant. Add the broccoli, bell pepper, and snap peas. Stir-fry for 4-5 minutes until the vegetables are crisp-tender.
4. **Combine:** Return the cooked chicken to the pan with the vegetables. 
5. **Thicken Sauce:** Give the sauce mixture a quick stir (the cornstarch may have settled) and pour it into the pan. Stir continuously for 1-2 minutes until the sauce has thickened and coated everything evenly.
6. **Serve:** Serve hot over steamed jasmine rice or noodles. Top with sesame seeds or sliced green onions if desired.
