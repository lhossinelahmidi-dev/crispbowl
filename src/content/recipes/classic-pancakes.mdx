## Introduction

There is nothing quite like a stack of warm, fluffy pancakes on a weekend morning. This recipe is my absolute go-to when I want classic diner-style pancakes without any fuss. They are incredibly tender, rise beautifully on the griddle, and soak up maple syrup like a dream. 

You probably already have all the ingredients in your pantry. The secret to their fluffiness is making sure your baking powder is fresh and not overmixing the batter!

## Instructions

1. **Dry Ingredients:** In a large bowl, sift together the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.
2. **Wet Ingredients:** Make a well in the center and pour in the milk, egg, and melted butter. Mix until perfectly smooth.
3. **Heat the Griddle:** Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat. You can test if it's hot enough by flicking a few drops of water onto the surface; they should dance and sizzle.
4. **Cook:** Pour or scoop the batter onto the griddle, using approximately 1/4 cup for each pancake. Brown on both sides and serve hot.
5. **Serve:** Top with additional butter and warm maple syrup. Enjoy!
