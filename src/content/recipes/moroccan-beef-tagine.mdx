## Introduction

This Moroccan Beef Tagine with Prunes is one of the most celebrated centerpieces of traditional Moroccan cooking. It strikes that unforgettable, magical balance between savory braised meat and sweet, spiced caramelized fruit that melts in your mouth.

Slowly simmered with ginger, saffron, and sweet onions, the meat turns spoon-tender while building a glossy, deeply flavored sauce.

## Instructions

1. **Brown the Beef:** In a heavy tagine or Dutch oven, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add the meat, grated onions, garlic, ginger, turmeric, salt, and pepper. Brown gently for 10 minutes until onions soften.
2. **Slow Simmer:** Pour in the saffron water and add the cinnamon stick. Add enough warm water to barely cover the meat. Cover, bring to a gentle simmer, and cook on low for 1.5 to 2 hours until the meat is meltingly tender.
3. **Caramelize the Prunes:** In a small saucepan, simmer the prunes in 1 cup of water with honey and ground cinnamon for 10 minutes until plump and glossy.
4. **Assemble & Garnish:** Arrange the tender beef on a warm serving platter, spoon the rich reduced sauce over, top with the caramelized prunes, toasted almonds, and a sprinkle of sesame seeds. Serve with warm crusty bread!
