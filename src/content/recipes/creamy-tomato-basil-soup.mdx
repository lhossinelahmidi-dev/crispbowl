## Introduction

Nothing beats a steaming bowl of homemade roasted tomato soup paired with a gooey grilled cheese sandwich. Roasting the tomatoes and whole garlic beforehand concentrates their natural sweetness and gives the soup an incredible, restaurant-quality depth of flavor.

## Instructions

1. **Roast the Vegetables:** Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Arrange tomato halves, quartered onion, and garlic bulb on a rimmed baking sheet. Drizzle generously with olive oil, salt, pepper, and oregano. Roast for 35 minutes until lightly charred.
2. **Blend:** Squeeze the sweet roasted garlic cloves out of their skins. Transfer roasted tomatoes, onions, and garlic to a blender. Add fresh basil and vegetable broth. Blend until silky and smooth.
3. **Simmer with Cream:** Pour the soup into a pot over medium-low heat. Stir in heavy cream and simmer for 5 minutes. Season to taste.
4. **Serve:** Ladle into bowls, drizzle with a touch of olive oil, and serve with crispy sourdough croutons!
