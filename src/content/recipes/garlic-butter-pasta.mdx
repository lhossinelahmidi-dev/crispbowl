## Introduction

When you're short on time but craving something comforting and savory, this Quick Garlic Butter Pasta is the perfect solution. It relies on pantry staples and comes together in the time it takes to boil water and cook the pasta. 

The magic happens when the starchy pasta water emulsifies with the melted garlic butter and Parmesan, creating a silky, glossy sauce that coats every strand of spaghetti.

## Instructions

1. **Cook Pasta:** Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the pasta and cook according to the package instructions until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water before draining.
2. **Prepare Sauce:** While the pasta is cooking, melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. 
3. **Sauté Garlic:** Add the minced garlic and sauté until fragrant, about 1-2 minutes. Be careful not to let the garlic brown or burn, as it will turn bitter. Add a pinch of red pepper flakes if using.
4. **Combine:** Add the drained pasta directly to the skillet with the garlic butter. Toss to combine.
5. **Emulsify:** Sprinkle the Parmesan cheese over the pasta and pour in 1/4 cup of the reserved pasta water. Toss vigorously until a smooth sauce forms, adding more pasta water if needed.
6. **Garnish & Serve:** Season with salt and lots of black pepper. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve immediately.
