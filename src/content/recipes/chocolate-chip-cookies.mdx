## Introduction

Everyone needs a reliable, perfect chocolate chip cookie recipe in their arsenal, and this is it. These cookies hit all the right notes: they have buttery, slightly crisp edges, wonderfully chewy centers, and pools of melted chocolate throughout.

The key to their amazing texture is the ratio of brown to white sugar, and making sure your butter is softened to room temperature, but not melted. Let's get baking!

## Instructions

1. **Preheat & Prep:** Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Line two large baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone baking mats.
2. **Dry Ingredients:** In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.
3. **Cream Butter & Sugars:** In a large mixing bowl or stand mixer, beat the softened butter, granulated sugar, brown sugar, and vanilla extract until light and fluffy (about 2-3 minutes).
4. **Add Eggs:** Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Scrape down the sides of the bowl as needed.
5. **Combine:** Gradually beat in the flour mixture on low speed until just combined. Do not overmix! 
6. **Chocolate:** Fold in the chocolate chips using a spatula or wooden spoon.
7. **Scoop:** Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto the prepared baking sheets, spacing them about 2 inches apart.
8. **Bake:** Bake for 9 to 11 minutes or until golden brown. The centers will still look slightly soft.
9. **Cool:** Let the cookies cool on the baking sheets for 2 minutes before transferring them to wire racks to cool completely.
